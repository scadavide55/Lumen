Free browser for macOS, version 1.6.4

A quiet browser for the Mac.

Tacet is a small, fast browser built on WebKit, the engine behind Safari. It blocks ads and trackers before they load and turns the blocker off for a site in one click. An assistant can read the page, your tabs and your history, and it can run entirely on your Mac. No account, no analytics, free and open source.

Features

Safari's engine, the things Safari is missing, and nothing you have to turn off.

Tabs that tidy themselves

Drag a tab onto another to make a group, or let Tacet group tabs from the same site. Save a group and close it, reopen it later with every tab. ⌘-click picks several, sort by site or title, close duplicates in one click.

Split view and Reader

Two pages side by side with ⌥⌘S. A cluttered article becomes clean text with ⇧⌘R, and the Reading List keeps it for the plane.

Ads blocked, natively

Stopped before they load by WebKit's own content blocker. No extension, no slowdown, and it works offline.

Light on memory

Tabs you haven't touched in a while go to sleep, then wake up exactly where you left them, history included.

Private by default

Tracking stripped from links, automatic HTTPS, Global Privacy Control, and private windows that forget everything and lock with Touch ID.

Passwords with Touch ID

Saved in the Mac's Keychain, filled after Touch ID, strong ones suggested on sign-up. Works beside 1Password and KeePassXC.

Tacet Plugins

A store of plugins checked by the developer: remove picture backgrounds offline with Cutout, a focus timer, smart dark mode, YouTube without Shorts, and more.

Extensions

Add the Safari extensions that come with your apps, or Chrome and Firefox ones from a file. You see what each can do before it runs.

Sites as apps

Install any site as an app with its own window and Dock icon, kept apart from your browsing.

Customize Site

Give any site its own theme, font and width, hide floating bars or anything you click on, add your own CSS or JavaScript.

Moving in is easy

Bookmarks, history and sign-ins come over from Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, Opera and Arc. Guided setup in English, Italian and French.

Updates itself

Tacet checks for new versions and installs them for you. Each one opens with a short note on what changed.

Command bar

Addresses, searches, tabs, bookmarks, history and commands, in one bar.

Press ⌘L or ⌘K in Tacet. The same bar is below, with a few tabs and bookmarks made up for it. Type a site to open it, a word to search, or the name of a command.

Shield

Ad blocking with a real off switch.

Most blockers are all or nothing. Shield has a switch for every site and sensible defaults everywhere else. ⇧⌘B turns the whole thing off and on again. The switches on the right work.

  • Works offline. A built-in list covers the biggest ad and tracking networks from the first launch.
  • Community lists. EasyList and EasyPrivacy add tens of thousands of rules, converted to WebKit rules and refreshed weekly.
  • No cookie walls. Consent banners are hidden and the page scrolls again.
  • Fewer YouTube ads. Video ads are stripped or skipped.
  • Third-party cookies blocked. Sites can't follow you around the web.

Clean links

Links arrive without their tracking tags.

Shared links carry tags that tell advertisers where you came from. Tacet strips them before the page loads, so you land on the same page, minus the tracking.

  • Removes utm_, fbclid, gclid, msclkid and dozens more
  • Knows the site-specific ones on YouTube, Spotify, Amazon and X
  • “Copy Link Without Tracking” in every page's menu
  • Upgrades to HTTPS, with an HTTPS-only mode if you want it strict

Assistant beta

Ask the page you're on.

⇧⌘A opens a panel beside any page, and the page comes along. The assistant can read your other tabs, history and bookmarks, the transcript of a YouTube video, and look things up on the web. With Browser use turned on it clicks and types for you.

  • Apple's on-device model on macOS 26: free, offline, no key needed. Or your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or DeepSeek, or a local model in Ollama or LM Studio.
  • Web access searches the web, reads pages, and lists its sources under the answer.
  • Browser use is off until you turn it on. It never touches password or card fields, and asks before sending, buying or deleting.
  • Select text, right-click: Explain, Translate, Summarise or Ask About It.
  • Chat history stays on your Mac. Text in web pages is treated as content, never as instructions.

Questions
What are Tacet Plugins?

Small plugins made for Tacet and checked by its developer, in a store inside the browser (⇧⌘P). They work on every Mac, are signed and checked file by file before they install, and run in a space of their own that pages can't reach. Cutout removes picture backgrounds on your Mac, offline; others hide YouTube Shorts, copy pages as Markdown, add a focus timer, darken light sites and more. Tacet Plugins is in beta.

Why did Lumen become Tacet?

There were already other browsers called Lumen, and the name sounded too much like an AI product. Tacet is the mark in music for silence, where an instrument stays quiet. It's the same browser: updating from Lumen keeps your tabs, passwords, settings and extensions.

Is Tacet free?

Yes. Tacet is free to download and use, and open source under the GPL-3.0 license.

Does Tacet collect any data?

No. There are no analytics and no accounts. Tacet only talks to the sites you visit. If you turn them on, it also downloads filter lists from easylist.to and fetches search suggestions from your search engine. Suggestions are off by default. Tacet Assistant (beta) is off too; if you turn it on, your questions and the page you attach go only to the AI provider you chose, with your own key. When it searches the web for you, the search goes to your provider (Anthropic) or to DuckDuckGo (other providers). With Apple's on-device model, nothing leaves your Mac.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Tacet isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may say Tacet “is damaged and can't be opened”. The download is fine. Open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tacet.app once, then open Tacet as usual. Tacet's own updates keep working after that.

Which engine does Tacet use?

WebKit, the same engine as Safari. Sites look and behave the way they do in Safari, and you get its speed and battery life.

Can I bring my bookmarks and passwords?

Yes. Tacet imports bookmarks, history and sign-ins straight from Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Opera, Brave, Edge and Arc. Passwords come across from a CSV exported by the Passwords app or your old browser, and are kept in your Mac's Keychain behind Touch ID.

Does Tacet support extensions?

Yes, on macOS 15.4 or later. Tacet runs web extensions, the same kind Safari, Chrome and Firefox use. Add the Safari extensions that come with apps on your Mac in one click from Settings › Extensions, or add a Chrome (.crx), Firefox (.xpi) or unpacked extension from a file. Most work; a few features only Chrome or Firefox have may not. Extensions never run in private windows.

What does the assistant cost?

Nothing from Tacet. With Apple's on-device model on macOS 26 it's free and offline. With Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or DeepSeek you pay the provider for what you use with your own key. With Ollama or LM Studio the model runs on your Mac.

Does it block every YouTube ad?

Most of them, most of the time. YouTube changes its ads often, so some may still get through.
